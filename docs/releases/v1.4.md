# Road Data Collector v1.4.0 — Session road ratings

## Changes

- Rate each session Very Bad, Bad, Good, or Very Good after stopping a recording, or skip and rate it later.
- Set or change the rating from Session Details. The session list shows the rating and color after returning from details.
- Display the session rating on the route map while preserving the existing road quality segment colors.
- Save the rating, numeric score (1–4), and rating timestamp in `metadata.json`.
- Include the rating and score at the top level and in every bucket of `correlation.json`. Changing a rating updates existing correlation data.
- Keep GPS and IMU CSV files unchanged. Session exports include the updated JSON automatically.
- Support older sessions without rating fields and allow the rating sheet to scroll on small screens.
- Add AT-011 for manual acceptance testing and 15 automated rating tests.
